Coeliac-safe rule

Use only foods that are naturally gluten-free or clearly labelled gluten-free. For packaged foods, sauces, stocks and processed products, verify the current label and avoid cross-contact.

Do not use: standard wheat bread, standard pasta, barley, rye, standard soy sauce, or regular oats. Use verified GF alternatives such as GF bread, GF pasta, rice noodles, certified GF stock and tamari.
Today's exchange boxes

Default allowance: 6 Protein, 3 Bread/Starch, 3 Fruit, 3 Fat, 2 Dairy. Tap boxes or use ±½ controls.

Free foods

Non-starchy vegetables and zero-exchange flavourings do not use exchange boxes. Potatoes, sweet potatoes, corn and peas are starches, not free vegetables.
