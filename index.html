Welcome to your Tracker!

This app makes eating well very easy. You do not need to count calories. Instead, you spend "Boxes".

Think of a Box like a token. Your daily budget gives you tokens for Proteins, Breads/Starches, Fruits, Fats, and Dairy.

  • When you eat a food, you check off a box.
  • When you run out of boxes for a category, you stop eating that type of food for the day.
  • Free Foods cost nothing. You can eat them any time you feel hungry!

How the Weeks Work

Your daily budget changes gently over time to help you progress.

  • Weeks 1 & 2: A starting point with more energy.
  • Weeks 3 & 4: Breads and starches step down slightly.
  • Weeks 5+: Fruits and fats adjust to a solid, steady routine.

Use the dropdown at the top to change your week. Note: Changing your week will reset today's boxes!

100% Gluten-Free

This whole app is built for Coeliacs. There is no wheat, no regular pasta, no soy sauce, and no oats (as oats are not Coeliac-safe in Australia). Every meal idea and food cost is totally safe for you.
