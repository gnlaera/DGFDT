Notes & GF Safety

Coeliac-safe rule

Use only foods that are naturally gluten-free or clearly labelled gluten-free. For packaged foods, sauces, stocks and processed products, verify the current label and avoid cross-contact.

Do not use standard wheat bread or pasta, barley, rye, malted gluten grains, standard soy sauce, couscous, semolina, bulgur/burghul, spelt, farro, freekeh or triticale. Oats are not included in this 100% GF tracker. Use verified GF alternatives such as GF bread, GF pasta, rice noodles, certified GF stock and tamari.

How exchanges are calibrated

  • Protein: about 7 g protein / 50–55 kcal.
  • Bread/Starch: about 15 g carbohydrate / 80 kcal.
  • Fruit: about 15 g carbohydrate / 60 kcal.
  • Fat: about 5 g fat / 45 kcal.
  • Dairy: about 12 g carbohydrate + 8 g protein / 90 kcal.

Important classification notes

The tracker preserves the supplied simplified rule that listed non-starchy vegetables are zero-exchange foods. The canonical 1995 lists had a separate vegetable list, so this is an adapted protocol. Potato, sweet potato, corn and peas count as Bread/Starch, not Free.

The v1.3 catalogue contains 400 foods. Generic portions are calibrated to the supplied 1990s exchange thresholds and rounded to practical half-exchange increments. Australian Food Composition Database Release 3 is used as a reference framework where practical; packaged foods remain label-sensitive because formulation and serving size vary.

This is a tracking protocol, not an individual medical or nutrition prescription.
